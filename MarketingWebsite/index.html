Our Intelligent Product Suite

Tools designed to streamline every stage of the architectural workflow.

Archion Sim

Archion Sim uses AI-driven agents to show how people move inside your building. It highlights congestion, accessibility issues, and overall flow quality using heatmaps.

Archion Sim Learn More

Archion Build

Archion Build instantly transforms your 2D floor plans into accurate 3D models. It removes the need for slow manual modeling and gives you a ready to edit structure.

Archion Build Learn More

Archion View

Archion View allows anyone to explore 3D designs directly in the browser with smooth controls. Users can rotate, zoom, cut sections, and review details without installing software.

Archion View Learn More

Archion Share

Archion Share lets you share your 3D models safely with flexible permission settings. You can choose private, view-only, or public access while keeping control of your work.

Archion Share Learn More
